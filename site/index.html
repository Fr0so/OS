DANMARK VM 26 - NOTES FROM THE BIN

World Cup expanded to 48 teams and Denmark still found a trapdoor. That is not a campaign, that is performance art with shin pads.

Czechia went through after a 2-2 playoff final and penalties. Cool. Normal. Meanwhile Denmark managed to turn qualification into a boardroom fog machine.

The worst part is not even losing. It is the tone afterwards. Process. Evaluation. Trust. All the usual words that mean nobody wants to say the football looked small, scared and over-managed.

Peter Møller backing Brian Riemer reads like one of those federation statements that has been printed before the match is even over. Very calm. Very professional. Very useful if the goal is to miss a 48-team World Cup with a straight face.

Fans online had the right energy: how do you have this player pool, this many chances, this much infrastructure, and still end up watching the tournament like the rest of us? At some point “bad luck” becomes a spreadsheet wearing a DBU polo.

Conclusion: not deleted. Archived out of shame.

MARCH MADNESS 26 - DUKE FAN DAMAGE REPORT

I have watched Duke lose March games before. I have processed pain. This was not pain. This was a software crash at center court.

Up big. Control of the game. One seed. Blue blood. All the ingredients for “handle business and get to the Final Four.” Then UConn turns the last minute into a horror short and Braylon Mullins hits the kind of logo three that will be replayed until I am elderly.

People are calling it cosmic payback, Laettner in reverse, whatever. Fine. Let the neutrals have their little poetry. As a Duke fan, all I saw was a team with the game in its hands deciding the basketball was radioactive.

And yes, everyone online is doing the same joke: just hold the ball. Just get fouled. Just do the one boring thing. The whole season came down to “please be normal for ten seconds,” and somehow that was too much.

The worst part is knowing this is now March Madness content forever. Not a loss. A clip. A meme. A freeze-frame. Every tournament package will dig it up and make me relive the exact moment my living room went silent.

Final note: I still love this stupid team. That is the problem.
