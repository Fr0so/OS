Forty-eight teams. Forty-eight. And Denmark still found a way to miss the World Cup. That takes real effort.

MARCH MADNESS 26

Duke had the game won and then completely lost its mind. That is the whole story.

All they had to do was hold the ball, take the foul, and be boring for ten seconds. Instead they played like the ball was radioactive and handed UConn a forever highlight.

As a Duke fan, that is the part that makes me sick. Not the shot. Not the noise. The fact that we basically opened the door for them ourselves.

So yeah, I was livid. This is not just a loss now. It is a clip, a meme, and a collapse people are going to replay every March until the end of time.
